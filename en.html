Corporate photography and video in Rio de Janeiro

Executive portraits, industrial and architecture photography, corporate video and event coverage, for companies and organizers working in Rio.

Cofy Film is a photo and video production company based in downtown Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Photographer and director Paulo H. Lima has worked in photography since 2001 and founded Cofy Film in 2014 to produce images for companies. Clients include OLX, Reserva, Nortec and the NBA.

You can brief us in English, by WhatsApp or e-mail. Quotes are tailored to each project, per day or per project.

Events: what to send us

For conferences, conventions and corporate events, send us the dates, venue, schedule and the deliverables you need: photographers, an editor for same-day galleries, videographers and interviews, or a highlight video. Standard delivery is within 5 business days, and express delivery is available on request.

How to request a quote

Tell us the date and venue, the type of work (portraits, industrial, architecture, video or event), how many people or hours, and when you need the files. We reply with the next steps.
